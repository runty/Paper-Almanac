# Paper Almanac changelog

## Unreleased

- Added **Kin’un**, a yellow Japanese tear-off calendar for Mac, iPhone, iPad, and widgets. A gilt octagonal mirror sits on the backing board. Vermilion gourds and rice sprigs mark 一粒万倍日 on the printed page.
- On Mac, drag the exposed backing board of **Kin’un** or **Tsukigoyomi** to move the calendar. The binding strip also moves the window, and dragging the sheet lifts it for tearing.
- Added **Tsukigoyomi**, an indigo-backed lunar calendar for Mac, iPhone, iPad and widgets. The page pairs the Moon at noon in Japan with the current solar term, a bilingual microseason reading, and the day’s Rokuyō. CarPlay shows a compact version.
- Added **Illyeok**, a Korean tear-off calendar with patterned date numerals, a lunar date, and 31 proverbs in Korean and English. The theme is available on Mac, iPhone, iPad, and their widgets, including CarPlay.
- Added **Rekichū**, a Japanese tear-off theme for Mac, iPhone, iPad, and widgets including CarPlay. Its full page shows Rokuyō, the old-calendar date, the daily mansion, lunar age, and a traditional tide name.
- Expanded the daily Japanese sayings from 16 to 140. Rekichū shows each saying with its English reading on the full page. Shōwa and Koyomi draw from the same rotation.

- Added **Tsukikage** to the Mac, iPhone, and iPad apps and their widgets. Its stippled lunar disk follows the daily phase. Dark-sky dates feature constellation drawings, and selected spaceflight anniversaries feature illustrated mission notes.
- Tsukikage opens with northern mid-latitude sky notes. Enable **Use Current Location** in the app for a local evening guide. Turning it off clears the saved location.

## 0.2.3 · November 18, 2026

- The new **Extra Large Portrait** widget fills a tall tile with one complete, borderless almanac page in your selected theme. Add it from the Home Screen on iPhone or from Today View on iPad.
- About features the new Wadscorp dog engraving and a matching Times New Roman wordmark. The GitHub link sits beside the Paper Almanac title.

## 0.2.1 · November 18, 2026

- Turn off **Auto Change Date** to hold the current page across midnight and relaunch. Tearing advances the held page one day. **Today** holds the current date when automatic changes are off.
- On iPhone and iPad, the palette groups **Go to Date…**, **Auto Change Date**, and **About**. Tap the large date number or choose **Today** inside the date picker to jump to today.
- On Mac, opening the menu-bar calendar raises the pad. **Auto Change Date** sits below **Calendar Size**, and the quit command reads **Quit**.
- The default theme is named **Yut Lik**. About links to the Paper Almanac source on GitHub.
- The app and widgets build the daily almanac engine and offline data from the included Swift package.
